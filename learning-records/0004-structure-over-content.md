# A complete set of material is not a course until the path is explicit

After the full nine lessons and five challenges were written, Sandor's reaction was not about the content but about navigation: "it is challenging to understand where to start, how to proceed with the challenges."

The material was complete and the structure was not. Lessons and challenges had separate numbering schemes, separate index pages, and no stated relationship — the reader had to work out that Challenge 01 belonged after Lesson 03. Reorganised on 26 Oct 2026 into one `course/` folder with a single index and 14 numbered steps, challenges placed directly after the lesson that equips them, and a Next link on every page.

**Implications:** when adding material, the navigation is part of the deliverable, not a follow-up. Two numbering schemes in one body of work is a smell. Watch for the same failure in the exercises themselves if the repo grows past two. Related: the pacing note in NOTES.md and [[MISSION.md]].

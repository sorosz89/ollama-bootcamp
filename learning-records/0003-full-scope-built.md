# Full course built before any of it was worked through

Sandor asked for the complete scope up front — "I will start learning when the full scope is done" — so all nine lessons, five reference sheets and the root index were written in one pass on 26 Oct 2026, rather than one lesson per session as the teaching loop normally runs.

**What this costs.** The normal loop picks each lesson from evidence of what the previous one established. There is no such evidence here: lessons 03–09 were pitched from the two data points available (fluent with cloud APIs; Lesson 02 was too dense before its Part 1 was added). The level is therefore a *guess applied nine times*, and the two-tier shape is the hedge against it being wrong.

**What to do about it.** Ask for calibration after the first worked lesson rather than waiting to be told, and expect to retrofit. The failure mode to watch for is the Lesson 02 one — leading with instruments before the idea.

**Implications:** the course can now be revised against real reactions instead of designed against assumptions, which is the better position. See [[MISSION.md]] and the pacing note in NOTES.md.

Build: the BriefedRecipe Zod schema.

It must:

  • Keep everything the recipe already carries — title, servings, ingredients, steps.
  • Add all six new fields.
  • Use enums wherever the value comes from a known set. Free text where a list would do is a bug here, not a style preference.
  • Contain nothing the code could derive. Total time is prep plus cook; asking the model for it buys you a chance of it being wrong.
  • Stay within 2 levels of nesting and 16 leaf fields. Both budgets come from Lesson 05's measurements, where a deep schema cost 4× the tokens and then failed.
  • Actually validate a plausible recipe.

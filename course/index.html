How this course works

  • Everything is measured, and says so. Figures carry the machine, model and date they came from. Where a claim is folklore, it is labelled folklore.
  • Mistakes stay visible. Step 8 opens by picking apart a flawed experiment from Step 2. Corrections teach more than a clean record.
  • Null results count. "No effect at 8k tokens" is written down, because almost nobody writes that down.
  • Ask at any point. Every page ends by inviting questions, and that is not decoration — it is the fastest part of this.
